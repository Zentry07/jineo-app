Questions

The honest FAQ.

How does the skin scan work?

You take one selfie. It’s analyzed once by an AI vision model to read cosmetic skin signals — hydration and oil balance, pore zones, marks and redness by facial zone, and tone for Personal Color. Your selfie is never stored on our servers.

What is Skin Type 16?

Our Korean-named skin identity system, built on the dermatologist-developed Baumann framework used in Korean skincare research. Four axes — Dry/Oily · Sensitive/Resistant · Pigment-prone/Non-pigmented · Wrinkle-prone/Tight — give you a 4-letter type and its Korean name.

Are the scores sponsored or paid by brands?

No. Zero paid rankings, zero sponsored scores. No brand can pay for placement or a better number, anywhere in the app. Product links may earn us an affiliate commission at no extra cost to you — commissions never touch scores.

Is Jineo free?

The first scan is free. Jineo Pro — re-scans, the Hana coach, Routine Roast, cycle tuning — is $5.99/week or $59.99/year with a 7-day free trial on annual. Managed through your Apple ID; cancel anytime.

Does Jineo diagnose acne or skin conditions?

No. Jineo reads cosmetic signals and suggests cosmetic routines — it doesn’t diagnose, treat, or cure anything. For persistent or concerning skin issues, please see a licensed dermatologist.

Is my selfie stored? Is it private?

Your selfie is analyzed once and never stored on our servers or sold. We ask explicit consent before every scan, never identify faces, and never produce a “looks score.” Full details in the privacy policy.
