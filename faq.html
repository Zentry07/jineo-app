What is Jineo?

A Korean skincare decision app for iPhone. A few honest answers and one selfie name your Korean Skin Type — one of five, not a number. Jineo builds a routine from products you already own and gives a straight answer on any product, Korean or Western, before you buy it. Plus Hana, a K-beauty coach you can chat with. No scores, and no brand money near a verdict.

How does the skin read work?

A few honest answers, then one selfie. It's analyzed once by an AI vision model to read cosmetic skin signals — hydration and oil balance, sensitivity, redness — and Jineo names your Korean Skin Type. Your selfie is never stored on our servers, and there is never a "looks score." It's a cosmetic guide, not a medical diagnosis.

What is a Korean Skin Type?

The way Korean skincare actually describes skin: five named types — Barrier-Recovery Sensitive (장벽회복 민감형), Inner-Dry Sensitive (속건조 민감형), Dehydrated Combination (수분부족 복합형), Outer-Oil Inner-Dry (겉지성 속건조형), Balanced Resilient (균형 건강형). A routine lens, not a diagnosis, and never a number.

What is a routine from what you own?

Jineo builds your morning and evening from the products already on your shelf — Korean or Western — and only then suggests what is missing. Every product check is decided against your skin, your shelf and your routine, before any shopping link is chosen.

Are the verdicts sponsored or paid by brands?

No. Zero paid rankings, zero sponsored placements. No brand can pay for a better verdict anywhere in the app. Product links may earn us an affiliate commission at no extra cost to you — commissions never touch a verdict. That rule is the product.

Is Jineo free?

Jineo Pro is $5.99/week or $59.99/year with a 7-day free trial on annual. The trial opens your named skin type, a routine from what you own, and product checks before you buy. Restore, settings, privacy controls and your data are always reachable. Managed through your Apple ID; cancel anytime.

Is my selfie stored? Is it private?

Your selfie is analyzed once and never stored on our servers or sold. We ask explicit consent before every scan, never identify faces, and never produce a "looks score." Full details: privacy policy.

Does Jineo diagnose acne or skin conditions?

No. Jineo reads cosmetic signals and suggests cosmetic routines — it doesn't diagnose, treat, or cure anything. For persistent or concerning skin issues, please see a licensed dermatologist.

Which products does Jineo know?

2,000+ real products from 100+ brands — Beauty of Joseon, Anua, COSRX, Sulwhasoo, Laneige, Torriden, Round Lab — plus the Western half of a mixed shelf: CeraVe, The Ordinary, Cetaphil, La Roche-Posay, Paula's Choice. Full ingredient lists on file. When Jineo doesn't know a product, it says so instead of guessing, and you can photograph the ingredient panel to have it added.

What does 물광 (mul-gwang) mean?

Korea has distinct words for distinct glow goals: 물광 mul-gwang = wet water glow · 속광 sok-gwang = lit-from-within · 윤광 yoon-gwang = satin soft glow · 꿀광 kkul-gwang = honey glow · 촉촉 chok-chok = moist and plump. The in-app glossary decodes 84 K-beauty terms, and your scan sets which glow your routine should chase.
